# Internal Testing Notes (Dry Run)

Template for the Week 3 internal dry run (handbook section 38), before real usability testing with external participants in Week 4.

**Date:** 04 August 2026
**Tester (acting as "the user"):** Project team member
**Observers:** Other project team members

## Task 1 — Use the AI assistant to find the correct onboarding document

* **Completed without help / with hesitation / failed:** Completed without help, with minor hesitation.
* **Issues noticed:** The AI assistant flow was generally clear, but the user took a short moment to understand which document or option was the most relevant. The available choices could be made slightly clearer to reduce hesitation.

## Task 2 — Find the correct person to contact for an IT issue

* **Completed without help / with hesitation / failed:** Completed with minor hesitation.
* **Issues noticed:** The support suggestion was understandable, but the user needed a short amount of time to identify the recommended person and understand why that person was suggested. The recommendation could benefit from clearer supporting information.

## Task 3 — Complete Day 1 tasks and understand what's next

* **Completed without help / with hesitation / failed:** Completed without help.
* **Issues noticed:** The Day 1 task flow was clear and the user could understand what needed to be completed next. However, clearer completion feedback would make it easier to confirm that a task had been successfully completed.

## General observations

* **Confusing labels:** Some labels and actions could be more explicit, particularly around support suggestions and confirmation of completed actions.
* **Dead ends:** No major dead ends were identified during the dry run. The main user journeys could be completed.
* **Anything unclear about the smart reminders or support suggestion features:** The features were generally understandable, but the purpose of some recommendations could be explained more clearly. Users should be able to understand why a particular reminder or support suggestion is being shown.

## Fixes made as a result

* Reviewed the onboarding flow to make the main actions and navigation clearer.
* Improved the clarity of confirmation and success states.
* Added clearer feedback for important actions so users can understand when an action has been completed.
* Added **Task Completed**, **Request Submitted**, and **Notification** confirmation states to strengthen the feedback provided to users.
* Reviewed the support suggestion flow to make the recommended contact easier to understand.
* Checked the main prototype flow again before proceeding to external usability testing.

**Note:** The internal dry run identified small areas for improvement but did not reveal any major blockers. The prototype was therefore considered suitable for the external usability testing stage. The later confirmation and notification states were added after the initial external testing and were not validated by the four external participants.
