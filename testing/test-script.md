
# Usability Test Script — Smart Onboarding
 

## Intro script (read to participant)

"Thank you for joining. This is a short usability test for a Smart
Onboarding prototype we've been designing as part of a university project
with HiveMind. There are no right or wrong answers — we're testing the
design, not you. Please think aloud as you go. With your permission, I'd
like to record this session."

## Warm-up questions
1. What's your current role, and roughly how long have you been there?
2. In one word, how would you describe your own onboarding experience?

---

## Task 1 — Use the AI assistant to find the correct onboarding document

**Instruction to participant:** "You need to find the current version of
the company's IT setup guide. Show me how you'd find it using the app."

**Success measure:** User finds the correct document and source link
within 2 minutes.

**Feedback question:** "How confident were you that this was the correct,
up-to-date version?"

---

## Task 2 — Find the correct person to contact for an IT issue

**Instruction to participant:** "Your laptop won't connect to the company
VPN. Show me how you'd find out who to contact."

**Success measure:** User finds the correct contact within 30 seconds.

**Feedback question:** "Was it clear who you should contact?"

---

## Task 3 — Complete Day 1 tasks and understand what's next

**Instruction to participant:** "Imagine today is your first day. Show me
what you'd do first, then show me how you'd know what to do next."

**Success measure:** User identifies their top priority task within 1
minute, and correctly explains what's done and what's next.

**Feedback question:** "Did the app make it clear what you should focus
on, and what was left to do?"

---

## Wrap-up questions
1. What felt clear? What felt confusing?
2. Would this have helped during your own first week at work? Why or why not?
3. Is there anything you'd change?
